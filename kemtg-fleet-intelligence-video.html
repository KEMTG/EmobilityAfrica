The value: spot a dead vehicle
before it costs you a week.

An idle vehicle earns nothing and hides in the data. Fleet Intelligence separates a rider waiting for a fare from a vehicle that has gone dark, and warns you up to 3 days ahead.

Earlier warning means faster repairs, fewer lost riding days and better lease and swap planning.
